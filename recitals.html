Recitals

Twice a year, the students take the stage.

Organized by Emma Hewitt. Nobody is forced on stage and nobody is left off. A first recital is often thirty seconds long, and that counts.

Who plays

Any current student who wants to.

Who comes

Anyone. It's free.

When

The next date is announced in the store and on Facebook.

How it works

  1. The teacher picks the piece

    Something the student can already play, not a stretch.

  2. It gets polished

    Not learned from scratch. Weeks of making a good thing better.

  3. They play it

    Sheet music on the stand is fine.

  4. Six months later

    They do it again, a little bigger.
