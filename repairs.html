What those words mean

Action

How high the strings sit above the neck. Too high and it's hard to press down. Too low and it buzzes.

Intonation

Whether the guitar stays in tune as you play higher up the neck. If chords sound sour up there, this is why.

The seasons

Wood swells and shrinks with the weather. A guitar that played great in spring can feel off by fall. A setup fixes it.
