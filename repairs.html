A setup, in plain terms

What a setup actually changes.

Action is how far the strings sit above the fretboard. Too high and the instrument fights you; too low and it buzzes. A setup dials that in.

Intonation is whether the instrument stays in tune as you move up the neck. If chords sound fine at the first fret and sour at the twelfth, that is intonation, and it is fixable.

Wood moves with the seasons. A guitar that felt perfect in June can feel wrong in December, and that is normal, not damage.
