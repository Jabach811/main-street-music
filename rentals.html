School band

Rent-to-own, so the money is not wasted if they stick with it.

Band instruments go out on a monthly rental. A share of what you pay goes toward owning the instrument outright, so a year of renting is not a year of throwing money at a rental company.

If the student switches instruments — and plenty do — come talk to us rather than eating the loss.

Monthly rates depend on the instrument and its condition. Call for a current quote before you commit.

  • Serviced in house. Our own repair tech looks at rentals when they come back and before they go out.
  • Local pickup. No shipping, no waiting for a box, no return label.
  • Real people. If something goes wrong you talk to the person at the counter, not a call center.
