Lessons

One teacher, one student, same time every week.

Teaching has been the heart of this store for three decades. You pick a day and a time, and that slot is yours — week after week, with the same teacher, so progress actually compounds.

We run the scheduling and the paperwork so our teachers can spend the half hour teaching.

$135–$185 per month, depending on the teacher
See how lessons work
  • Eight instrument families. Piano and keyboard, guitar and bass, ukulele, voice, brass, woodwinds, drums and percussion, and orchestral strings.
  • Teachers who still gig. Full-time musicians and educators. Most hold music degrees; many hold California teaching credentials.
  • Recitals twice a year. Students play for a real audience, not just a parent in a hallway.
  • Every age, every level. First-timers through advanced players, children through adults.
  • Holidays off. The lesson calendar marks the weeks we’re closed, so nobody pays for a week they can’t use.
