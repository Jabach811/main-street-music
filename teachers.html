Teachers

Fourteen working musicians.

Trained at Berklee, Manhattan School of Music, the San Francisco Conservatory and the Brubeck Institute. They play in bands, orchestras and operas. Most have taught here for a decade or more.

  • Mike Hall

    Piano & Hammond B3

    63 years on piano. Performing since 1968 and teaching since 1972. He's opened for Hot Tuna and Jefferson Starship and played the Monterey Jazz Festival.

    Also teaches flute, piccolo, guitar, bass, ukulele and banjo.

  • Takako Williams

    Piano

    Started at four in Osaka. Studied at Osaka College of Music, Notre Dame de Namur and the San Francisco Conservatory, and won first prize in the Notre Dame concerto competition.

    26 years teaching.

  • Emma Hewitt

    Piano

    Nearly 20 years playing, trained in the Suzuki method. Joined in 2022.

    She runs both of the store's recitals every year.

  • Phoenix Thanatos

    Flute, piccolo, voice & piano

    Performance degree from Cal State East Bay and 14 years on flute. Played in an Amy Porter master class and sang in the chorus of the Legend of Zelda symphony.

    5 years teaching.

  • Brian Hunt

    Guitar, bass & ukulele

    Master's Certificate from Berklee College of Music.

    Teaching here since 2008.

  • Sean Britt

    Guitar

    The first guitarist ever picked for the Brubeck Institute Fellowship, in 2013. He's played with Christian McBride and Stefon Harris.

    BFA from The New School, master's from Manhattan School of Music.

  • Bernardette Medeiros

    Voice

    Graduated cum laude from University of the Pacific on a full scholarship. Past students have gone on to America's Got Talent, The Voice and American Idol.

    14 years teaching. Third-generation Tracy local.

  • Edward Tavalin

    Voice

    A baritone trained at University of the Pacific and Cal State Northridge. He's sung with Santa Cruz Opera Project, Portland Opera and Opera Modesto.

  • Tom Renner

    Sax, clarinet & flute

    30 years teaching. He coordinates visual and performing arts for Tracy Unified.

    Also teaches trumpet, trombone, violin and piano.

  • Dave Villa

    Sax & winds

    Playing since 1982, teaching since 1995, with 21 years in public school music programs. CSU Stanislaus.

    Also teaches flute, clarinet, trumpet, trombone, violin and viola.

  • Joseph Serrano

    Drums & percussion

    23 years behind the kit. He plays in the Latin funk band Third Sol and has toured the US and Canada with Wrath of Vesuvius and Black Passage.

    Funk, R&B, rock, jazz and metal.

  • Elizabeth Dubie

    Violin, viola & cello

    Teaching privately since 1996 and in Tracy schools since 2008. Music degree from CSU Stanislaus.

  • Erik Alvarez Urbina

    Cello

    Studied at University of the Pacific under Louise Saunders, Nina Flyer and Jakob Koranyi.

    Known around here as the Rocking Cellist.

  • Aidan

    Cello

    Came to Tracy from Milwaukee in 2023, where he was principal cellist of the Milwaukee Philharmonic and lead string instructor for the Milwaukee Youth Symphony.

    He also composes.
