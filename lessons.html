Lessons

One teacher. One student. Same time every week.

Private lessons in the rooms behind the showroom. You get a standing weekly slot with the same teacher, so they learn how you play and you actually get better.

What we teach

  • Piano & keyboard

    Classical through jazz and pop. Reading music or playing by ear.

  • Guitar & bass

    Acoustic, electric and upright. Chords, songs, theory, or all of it.

  • Ukulele

    The fastest way from zero to playing a real song.

  • Voice

    Breathing, range and confidence, from choir kids to stage singers.

  • Brass

    Trumpet and trombone. Band students welcome.

  • Woodwinds

    Flute, clarinet, saxophone and piccolo.

  • Drums & percussion

    The kit, hand drums and mallets. Funk, rock, jazz, metal.

  • Orchestral strings

    Violin, viola and cello.

Getting started

  1. Call or walk in

    (209) 835-1125, or stop by the counter any day we're open.

  2. Tell us three things

    The instrument, the student's age, and which afternoons work.

  3. Get matched

    We pair you with a teacher who has an opening that fits.

  4. Show up weekly

    Same day, same time, same teacher. Need an instrument? Rent or buy one at the counter.

How young?

It depends on the instrument. Call and we'll tell you honestly whether it's time.

Any level?

Yes. First-timers, returning players, and students getting ready for auditions.

Recitals?
