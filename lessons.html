Lessons

One teacher, one student, the same half hour every week.

You pick a day and a time. That slot is yours, week after week, with the same teacher — which is the only way progress actually adds up.

Call to book a slot Meet the teachers
What it costs

One monthly rate. No enrolment fee, no contract.

Lessons are billed by the month and cover a weekly private lesson. The range comes down to which teacher you study with — the most experienced teachers sit at the top of it.

The lesson calendar marks the weeks we are closed for holidays, so nobody pays for a week they cannot use.

$135–$185 per month, depending on the teacher

Rates for half-hour versus hour-long lessons, and for siblings enrolling together, are worth asking about on the phone.

  • Private, not group. Every lesson is one teacher and one student.
  • A standing slot. Same day, same time, same room, same teacher.
  • Every age and level. First-timers through advanced players, children through adults.
  • Recitals twice a year. Students play for a real audience, not just a parent in a hallway.
  • Instruments on hand. If you need one to start, you can rent or buy it at the front counter.
What we teach

Eight instrument families.

Who teaches what
  • Piano & keyboard. Classical through jazz and pop.
  • Guitar & bass. Acoustic, electric and upright.
  • Ukulele. A common first instrument for younger students.
  • Voice. Technique, breath and repertoire.
  • Brass. Trumpet, trombone and the rest of the section.
  • Woodwinds. Flute, clarinet, saxophone, piccolo.
  • Drums & percussion. Kit, hand drums and mallets.
  • Orchestral strings. Violin, viola, cello.
Getting started

The fastest way in is a phone call.

Tell us the instrument, roughly how old the student is, and which afternoons or evenings work. We will tell you which teachers have room and put you in the first open slot that fits.

Walking in works just as well. Somebody at the counter can check the schedule while you are standing there.
