Since 1993

1993

Ken Cefalo opens Main Street Music on June 1, at 53 West 10th Street.

2014

The store moves a few doors down to 45 West 10th, the old Lighting Gallery building.

2018

A piano goes out on the sidewalk for anyone to play. There's been one there ever since.

2023

Thirty years. Ken sits in with the Tracy Community Jazz Ensemble at 10th and Central to celebrate.

“We joke every year a couple slip through and become professional musicians.”

Ken Cefalo, to the Tracy Press, 2023. Tens of thousands of students have come through. Some now bring their own kids.
