Inside

What is actually in the building.

  • The showroom. Guitars, amps, keyboards, drums, brass and woodwinds, out where you can play them.
  • Lesson rooms. Private rooms where the weekly lessons happen, in the back.
  • The repair bench. Setups, restrings and electronics work, done on site by our own tech.
  • The counter. Strings, reeds, cables and everything else you ran out of on a Tuesday night.
First time

What to bring, depending on why you are coming.

None of this needs an appointment. Walk in during opening hours and somebody at the counter will sort you out.

  1. Signing a student up for lessons

    Bring the instrument if you already own one, and know roughly which afternoons or evenings are free. We check the schedule at the counter and put you in the first open slot that fits. How lessons work.

  2. Renting a band instrument

    Bring the school’s instrument list if the teacher sent one home, plus a photo ID and a card. Rent-to-own means the payments count toward owning it. What we rent.

  3. Dropping off a repair

    Bring the instrument and its case. Tell us what it is doing wrong, or hand it to the tech and let him find it. We quote before any work starts. Repair services.

  4. Just trying things out

    Bring nothing. Pick up whatever is on the wall, plug it in and play it for ten minutes. Nobody is going to hover. What we carry.
